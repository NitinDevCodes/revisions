About Our Institute

Welcome to KCCITM, a premier educational institution dedicated to providing top-quality technical and scientific education. Founded in 1995, we offer undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines.

Academic Departments

1. Department of Computer Science and Engineering

Head of Department: Dr. A. K. Sharma

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering focuses on core computing concepts, software development, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.

Programs Offered:

  • B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering
  • M.Tech in Data Science
  • Ph.D. in Computer Science

Key Laboratories:

  1. Advanced Software Engineering Lab
  2. Artificial Intelligence & Robotics Lab
  3. Network & Security Lab

2. Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Head of Department: Prof. S. Ramanujan

This department covers modern power systems, signal processing, embedded systems, and renewable energy technologies.

Programs Offered:

  • B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
  • M.Tech in Power Electronics
  • Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering

Key Laboratories:

  1. Power Systems Lab
  2. Control Systems & Instrumentation Lab
  3. VLSI & Embedded Systems Lab

3. Department of Mechanical Engineering

Head of Department: Dr. R. P. Verma

Dedicated to foundational mechanical concepts, CAD/CAM, thermal engineering, and modern robotics applications.

Programs Offered:

  • B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering
  • M.Tech in Thermal Engineering
  • Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering

Key Laboratories:

  1. Fluid Mechanics Lab
  2. Automobile Engineering Lab
  3. CAD/CAM Manufacturing Lab
